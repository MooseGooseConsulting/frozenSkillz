# Example Corpus Synthesis Shape

This is an illustrative shape, not a required report template.

## Corpus and coverage

State the complete candidate count, the individually extracted corpus, exclusions, harness/date
coverage, skill versions, and blind spots. Make clear whether every deployment or only a selected
corpus was reviewed.

## What agents understood

Summarize how case readers explained the skill's purpose. Preserve meaningful disagreement rather
than selecting the most convenient restatement.

## Where the skill entered work

Describe common deployment points and timing. Separate task use from skill editing, inspection, and
meta-analysis.

## What appeared helpful

For each theme, cite supporting cases, counterexamples, the apparent helpful behavior, and what
remains unknown about attribution.

## What appeared harmful or wasteful

Describe scope expansion, confusion, context load, ceremony, unnecessary handoffs, or other effects
that emerged. Do not limit the synthesis to this list.

## Missing, vague, or overspecific guidance

Keep these distinct so the proposed repair targets the right part of the skill.

## Owner responses

Quote or tightly paraphrase explicit acceptance and correction with source pointers. Do not infer a
verdict from silence.

## Competing interpretations

Explain where readers disagreed, where the same pattern has a counterexample, and what additional
bounded case would discriminate between explanations.

## Improvement hypotheses

For each proposal, identify the affected skill surface, supporting cases, constraining
counterexamples, smallest change, and later observation or regression that would test it.

## What not to change yet

Record attractive but unsupported conclusions so they do not quietly become requirements.
